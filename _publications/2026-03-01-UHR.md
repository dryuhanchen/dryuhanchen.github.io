<div style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-word;">
<b>Abstract:</b> After the war, a number of gangs specializing in scalping theater tickets emerged near major theaters in Shanghai. These groups operated in considerable numbers. Their existence was closely intertwined with the police, theaters, and audiences, and the interactions among these three parties formed a dynamic equilibrium. This made it difficult for the three forces to unite in a concerted effort to eliminate the ticket-scalping gangs, allowing them to maintain a certain degree of space for survival. The gangs’ long-term persistence exposed the deficiencies of the legal system during the Republican period and highlighted the complexity and diversity of postwar grassroots society.
</div>
